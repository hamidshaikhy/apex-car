## Campaign photography

All twelve vehicle images in `editorial/`, plus `atelier-background.png`, are AI-generated campaign images created with the built-in image generation tool for this project. They are visual concepts, not photographs of actual inventory. Most generated vehicle sources are 1672 × 941; the final Challenger source is 1954 × 805. The modern Challenger replacement is stored non-destructively as `challenger-2009.webp`; the initial illustration remains unused. The architectural background contains no vehicles. The prompts and asset paths are recorded in `docs/IMAGE_PROMPTS.md`. Gallery detail views are crops of these same campaign images.
